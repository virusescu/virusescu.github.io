# 3D Art 2: the painting as a real 3D scene

Plan, written 2026-11-14. This is the follow-up to [3dart](../3dart/PLAN.md), which fakes depth by pushing a sphere in and out.

## Status (2026-11-14, evening)

Decided: local only (B1, then B2), a step of about 1 m, 360° from the start, personal and non-commercial.

**B1 is built as a full 360° scene** and runs in the viewer (`index.html`, `main.js`). The pipeline:

| Step | Tool | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `tools/sharp360.py` | Cuts 10 perspective views (8 around the horizon at 100°, one up, one down at 110°) from `3dart/scenes/winter-landscape/color.jpg` and runs SHARP on each with the exact focal length. About 90 s on the RTX 5080 |
| 2 | `tools/merge.py` | Aligns each view's scale to a ground plane 1.6 m below the eye (SHARP's scale drifts up to 4× between views), stops anything going below the ground (pulls river reflections onto the water), puts the sky on a 250 m dome, the up view on the dome and the down view on the ground, keeps each direction from one view only, thins the sky. 2.73 M splats |
| 3 | Spark `build-lod --quality --csplat --rad-chunked` | Precomputed level of detail, streamed: `world-lod.rad` plus 64 chunks of about 1.2 MB (69 MB in all). Spark picks about 1 M splats on Android, 1.5 M on iOS, 2.5 M on desktop |
| 4 | `tools/shots.py` | Screenshots from given positions through Edge headless on the GPU, for checking |

Commands (from `3dart2/`):

```
D:/Programs/ml-sharp/.venv/Scripts/python.exe tools/sharp360.py ../3dart/scenes/winter-landscape/color.jpg work/views
D:/Programs/ml-sharp/.venv/Scripts/python.exe tools/merge.py work/views work/world.ply
D:/Programs/spark-src/rust/target/release/build-lod.exe work/world.ply --quality --csplat --rad-chunked
```

Set `TORCH_HOME=D:/Programs/ml-sharp/torch-cache` for SHARP. Installed on the PC, all in `D:\Programs`: `uv` (Python 3.13), `ml-sharp` with PyTorch 2.8 CUDA 12.8, `shot-venv` (Playwright), Rust (GNU toolchain), `mingw64`, `spark-src` (for `build-lod`).

What it looks like: the stitch has no visible seams, trees occlude the far shore correctly, and at the edge of the 1 m bubble there are no holes. Weak spots: the river reflection becomes a flat streak on the water when seen from the side, and the snow right at the feet is soft.

Findings:

- **The front is the panorama's repaint, not the original canvas.** `color.jpg` was generated by Gemini, which repainted the painting and stretched its composition over about 200°. To have the real brushwork in front, the world has to be outpainted around the actual painting first (see Next).
- **SHARP's model license** allows "Research Purposes" only (non-commercial scientific research, experimentation, testing). A personal showcase is a grey area; any commercial use is out. Distribution of derivatives must carry the notice "Apple Machine Learning Research Model is licensed under the Apple Machine Learning Research Model License Agreement."
- Spark and three.js are both MIT.

## Goal

The visitor stands inside *Peisaj de iarnă* by Ecaterina Staicu and the scene is truly three-dimensional: trees stand in front of the far shore and hide it properly, you can lean and take a step, and nothing stretches like rubber. It should make people say "wow" on a phone.

## What changes compared with 3dart

| | 3dart (today) | 3dart2 |
|---|---|---|
| Scene | One sphere, displaced by a depth map | Gaussian splat: millions of small soft blobs of colour placed in space |
| Occlusion | None; edges stretch | Real; near things hide far things |
| Movement | Turn only (eye 12 cm off the pivot) | Turn, plus a step or two in any direction |
| What is behind a tree | Nothing, smeared | Invented by AI, but present |
| File size | About 1.2 MB | Roughly 10 to 40 MB, streamed |

Splats suit this painting: they are soft and slightly blurred by nature, which reads as brushwork instead of as a defect.

## Plan A: world generated by Marble

[Marble](https://marble.worldlabs.ai) by World Labs turns one image into a full explorable 3D world and exports it as a Gaussian splat.

**Does it cover extending the painting?** Yes. From a single image Marble invents the whole surroundings, including what is behind the viewer, so the separate outpainting step from 3dart is not needed. If its own extension drifts from the painter's style, the alternative is to give it the seam-fixed panorama we already have (`3dart/scenes/winter-landscape/color.jpg`); Marble is reported to accept panoramas as input, to be confirmed when we try.

### Steps

1. **Generate** (you, in the browser): upload `3dart/scenes/winter-landscape/source/painting.jpg` to Marble. Try two or three generations and keep the one closest to the painting.
2. **Export** the splat (`.spz` preferred, it is the compressed format; `.ply` also works) and put it in `3dart2/scenes/winter-landscape/`.
3. **Viewer** (me): copy the 3dart viewer and replace the displaced sphere with a splat renderer. Start screen, gyroscope, drag, text in space and sound stay.
4. **Movement**: allow a small bubble of movement, about one metre around the start point. On desktop with W/A/S/D or arrow keys, on the phone with a two-finger drag. Beyond the bubble the generated world falls apart, so the limit hides that.
5. **Tune**: start position and direction, splat count for phones, loading order.
6. **Publish** with `git push`, after you confirm.

### Renderer

[Spark](https://sparkjs.dev) is World Labs' open-source (MIT) splat renderer for three.js. It reads Marble's formats directly, streams large scenes with level of detail, and runs on phones. It needs three.js r180 or newer, so 3dart2 gets a newer three.js than 3dart. three.js r186 also added its own splat renderer, WebGPU only; Spark is the safer choice for phones today.

## Plan B: built on the PC (RTX 5080, 32 GB RAM)

No subscription, more control, more work. I need shell access to that PC: Claude Code installed on it, or SSH from the home server over Tailscale.

### B1. Local splat from the image

[SHARP](https://github.com/apple/ml-sharp) by Apple turns one image into about 1.2 million Gaussians in under a second on a normal GPU. Run on the painting it gives the same kind of file as Marble, viewed with the same viewer.

Limit: SHARP reconstructs what the image shows, a window of roughly 70°, and is good for small head movements only. It does not invent the world behind you. To get 360° we would run it on several views cut from the outpainted panorama and merge the results, which is experimental.

### B2. Blender stage set

Real geometry built in Blender by script, the painting projected onto it:

1. Split the painting into layers: sky, far tree line, right tree group, left tree group with the tall trunk, river, near bank, reeds.
2. Fill in what each layer hides (AI inpainting), so there is something behind every tree.
3. Build simple geometry: a ground mesh from the depth map, the river as a flat plane, each tree group as a cut-out card standing at its own distance, the sky as a dome.
4. Project the painting from the original viewpoint, bake textures, export glTF.
5. View in the 3dart viewer with a mesh instead of the sphere.

This keeps the artist's actual brush strokes better than any generator, with clean edges and small files (a few MB). It is theatre scenery, though: convincing within a step of the start point, flat if you could walk around it. Blender runs headless; I check my own renders as I go.

### B3. Blender as a finishing tool for Plan A

The likely real use of the PC: take Marble's splat, and in Blender (or SuperSplat) cut away broken regions, re-centre it, and bake a short fly-through video for sharing.
